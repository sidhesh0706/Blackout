# Blackout
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated by the user: Python standard-library HTTP server, SQLite and vanilla HTML/CSS/JavaScript. No installation needed beyond Python 3.11+.

## Users and purpose
Incident responders checking containment after an application restore, and technical evaluators reviewing a laptop prototype. Demonstrate that an older database can restore compromised access even while business health checks pass.

## Mechanism
Independent containment evidence and validated test requirements survive the application rollback. Real HTTP probes test denied access and legitimate business transactions. Results are PASS, FAIL or UNVERIFIED, scoped to the declared checks.

## Constraints
Synthetic accounts and incident evidence only. Real SQLite backups/restores, real application requests. AI proposes requirements but cannot authorize them or generate arbitrary executable tests. Optional Gemini integration; offline rule extraction must be labelled honestly. An operator explicitly approves correction. No claims of complete recovery safety, measured industry performance, insurer acceptance or Mitigata integration.

## Brand commitments
Blackout; the user explicitly replaced the original ivory deck direction with a dark cybersecurity interface, requested glow animation, and required general product branding without competition references. Code-first browser implementation approved. No demo video.

## Success
A judge can reproduce containment regression, inspect evidence, correct it and challenge the result. Clear distinction between observed outcomes and assumptions.
